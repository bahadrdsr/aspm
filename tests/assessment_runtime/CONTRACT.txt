Normal TLS/direct-client selection
---------------------------------
ASPM_ASSESSMENT_CA_FILE is optional; absent/empty selects normal system trust.
A supplied path must be a regular nonempty certificate-only PEM file <=1MiB,
strictly parsed with at least one valid certificate and no junk/private keys.
It is explicit trust for an owned/private inference endpoint, not a trust-store
change or disabled certificate validation. Do not read/download/discover a CA.

Environment constructs a bounded explicit *http.Transport client with Proxy=nil,
no cookie jar or redirect following, normal hostname/TLS verification and
effective TLS 1.2 or newer, no InsecureSkipVerify, ServerName override,
DialTLS/DialTLSContext or default-client fallback. Preserve valid TLS ranges.
The base client need not guess an endpoint: current persisted profile/grant
selects endpoint/model/key at dispatch, and the accepted worker binds origin/base
per request. ASPM_MODEL_ENDPOINT or caller headers are NOT new authority.
Reuse/factor the existing delivery/collection normal-client and bounded PEM
building pieces where appropriate, without their static gateway defaults.
Client timeout must be explicit, positive and <=30s; request deadline is the
configured AssessmentRequestTimeout. Owned resources close when the role ends.
