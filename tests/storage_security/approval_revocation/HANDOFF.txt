Four bounded scenarios
----------------------
1. A trusted, initially valid approval expires while Evidence.Read is blocked.
2. The trusted approval is revoked after that read starts.
3. The caller cancels during a blocked read, independently of approval policy.
4. Approval is revoked at evidence Close, after valid fixture data reaches EOF.

The reader never releases successful data in the blocked-read cases. It can
stop only when its operation context is cancelled or the reader is closed.
Expiry/revocation must actively terminate the read within two seconds of the
policy change, then finish verification without reopening/resuming evidence.
Finishing the read and discarding its result is not an acceptable substitute.
The constructor/caller deadline is ten seconds, outside that policy window;
the fixture itself does not implement cancellation or approval business logic.

Policy expiry/revocation: ErrPolicy, outcome=blocked, caller still active.
Caller cancellation: context.Canceled, outcome=cancelled, not ErrPolicy.
All stopped cases must close their reader and return no success artifact
references, false-positive classification or finding-closure decision.
The final-result case requires a fresh trusted LookupApproval AFTER evidence
EOF/Close, before success/artifacts can be returned. Reusing an old approved
snapshot is not sufficient.
